The paradigm, applied

One screen, one job at a time. Naming and dates are quick; the destination is where attention goes; everything AI/advanced waits politely until invited.

Destination is the hero. A full-width focused search with the AI-resolve pill and roomy 48px photo suggestions — not a cramped chip. It's the field people actually came to fill.

Dates collapse to one line that opens the scroller sheet — the calendar lives in the sheet, not inline, so the form stays short.

Advanced is a single slim row, off by default. It no longer pushes the destination down. Flip it on and a compact modes + legs panel reveals; tap a leg to open the editor.

One lime CTA + an escape hatch. "Draft my plan with AI" is the single primary action; "start empty" stays beside it. Lime is never spent on anything else here.

Rule learned from the reference: progressive disclosure — show the 90% path big and clean; tuck the 10% power path behind one tap.
